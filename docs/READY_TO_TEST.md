## 🚀 Quick Test Guide (5 minutes)

### Step 1: Login
1. Go to: http://localhost:3000/login
2. Login with your account
3. Or register at: http://localhost:3000/register

### Step 2: Access Research Tools
1. Click "Research Tools" in sidebar
2. Or go to: http://localhost:3000/dashboard/research

### Step 3: Test AI Generator
**Tab: AI Generator**

1. Enter topic: `machine learning in medical imaging`
2. Focus areas (optional): `CNN, diagnostic accuracy, radiology`
3. Click "Generate Research Summary"
4. Wait 5-10 seconds ⏳
5. 🎉 See AI-generated comprehensive summary!

**What you'll get:**
- Overview of the field
- 5 key research papers
- Major research themes
- Research gaps
- Future directions

### Step 4: Test PubMed Search
**Tab: PubMed Search**

1. Enter query: `deep learning cancer`
2. Click search 🔍
3. See 10 real papers from PubMed
4. Click "View on PubMed" to see full paper

## 🐛 If Something Doesn't Work

### AI Generator Error:
- Check `.env.local` has `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`
- Make sure you have Anthropic credits
- Check console for errors

### PubMed No Results:
- Try broader search terms
- Check internet connection
- Try different keywords

### Can't Access Page:
- Make sure you're logged in
- Check dev server is running
- Clear browser cache
